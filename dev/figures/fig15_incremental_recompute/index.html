    Figure 15 — Incremental recomputation with unit fingerprints¶

    Vector PDF

    File: fig15_incremental_recompute.pdf

    Suggested caption. Dependency graph of unit fingerprints. Each unit of work stores a hash of its inputs, its own settings, the relevant configuration files, and the fingerprints of the units it depends on. When a setting changes, only the units downstream of it are recomputed.

    What the figure shows¶

    • Roots: processed data (data_key: split, number of points, T), the generator training windows (max_windows, holdout_frac), and two committed JSON files (target_params*.json, model_configs.json).
    • Generator training and sampling are separate units. VAE fitting depends on the training windows; pool sampling depends on the fitted weights and the pool size. Diffusion-TS fitting depends on the training windows; the loads of the pretrained generators (Chronos, TimesFM, Timestral) depend only on the data.
    • Series of a method depend on their upstream generator units and on the settings of their assembler or joiner (e.g. mbb_bandwidth, pctsp_*, join_overlap).
    • Evaluation units: fidelity depends on the series and the target parameters (of its own dataset: the thresholds of a new dataset do not redo the others); labels depend on the data and the target parameters; utility (TSTR) depends on the series, the labels and the model configurations; TRTR depends on the labels and the model configurations only. Fidelity and utility units are fingerprinted per cell (metric group; task × model): the done cells are listed in a registry next to the unit (cells.json), so an added or changed model, task or metric group computes only its own cells.

    Example¶

    Changing knn_k (the number of neighbours of vae_knn) recomputes the vae_knn series (and its joiner variants, where they are run) together with their fidelity and utility. Pools, VAE weights, the other methods, labels, TRTR and the fidelity of the real series are kept. Changing the pool size re-samples pools from the stored weights and redoes every same-pool method, while VAE training, Diffusion-TS, the pretrained generators, labels, TRTR and real-data fidelity are kept.

    Notes for the text¶

    • This makes long runs resumable across sessions (e.g. Colab disconnections) and keeps all cached results consistent with the current configuration.
    • A utility unit in which every fit failed (e.g. out of GPU memory) is not marked done and is retried on the next run.
    • Source: marathonts/bench/config.py (unit_fingerprint), BENCHMARK.md "Stages".
