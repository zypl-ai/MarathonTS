    Licence terms that matter here¶

    • CC BY-ND 4.0 (ETTh1). The licence allows sharing the dataset unchanged, and producing adapted material, but not sharing adapted material (§2(a)(1)(B), §3(a)(2)). Adapted material is material derived from the dataset and modified "in a manner requiring permission under Copyright and Similar Rights". Whether numeric measurements, synthetic series sampled from a generator trained on them, or the generator weights are adapted material in this sense is not settled. This project takes the conservative reading and withholds them (next section).
    • CC BY-SA 4.0 (Bitcoin). Adapted material may be shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or a later version, or a licence listed as BY-SA compatible), with attribution and an indication of changes, and without additional terms or technical measures that restrict the rights the licence grants.
    • CC BY 4.0. Adapted material may be shared under any licence, with attribution, a link to the licence and an indication of changes.
    • CC0 1.0 (PJM). No conditions on the Kaggle copy. The primary source (PJM) restricts redistribution of its data and of data derived from them (see "Primary sources"); this project withholds the artifacts derived from PJM data on the same basis as ETTh1 (next section).

    Metrics, tables, rankings and plots computed from a dataset report results about the data and do not reproduce it; they are published for all nine datasets, with attribution.

    What is published¶

    <ds> is a dataset folder. "Derived from data" means the file contains values computed from the real series or from a model trained on it.

    "Dataset licence" means: under CC BY 4.0 with the attribution below. Withheld ETTh1 and PJM artifacts are reproduced locally by running the pipeline (GETTING_STARTED.md); everything that the hypothesis testing reads from them is regenerated by the same stages.

    Data files themselves (data/raw/, data/processed/) are not distributed for any dataset: the raw stage downloads them from the sources.

    Attribution and changes¶

    Synthetic series and other adapted material in artifacts/ are based on the datasets listed above, by their respective authors (see the table and DATASETS.md). Changes made to the data: one target column was taken, aggregated to an hourly step (mean, or last log price for Bitcoin), missing Bike hours were set to 0, Metro was taken after its 2014–2015 gap, gaps of up to 6 hours were interpolated, the last points of the series were taken (max_points; Bitcoin between bitcoin_start and bitcoin_end), the series was split in time and min-max scaled on train; synthetic series were generated by the methods of the benchmark. The data are provided as is, without warranties, as stated in the source licences.

    Primary sources¶

    The licence is set by whoever published the dataset; the primary data may have their own terms. Checked against the sources in October 2026.

    • PJM (#4): the Kaggle dataset states that the data come from the PJM website. PJM's current data service, Data Miner 2, states that redistribution of information or data contained in or derived from Data Miner 2 requires a PJM-issued Redistribution License, and its terms limit non-members to internal business use. The Kaggle dataset does not state through which PJM service the data were obtained. The benchmark does not distribute the PJM data (raw downloads them from Kaggle); whether these terms apply to artifacts derived from the Kaggle copy is a legal question for the publisher of the artifacts; this project does not distribute artifacts derived from PJM data ("What is published").
    • Bitcoin (#9): the Kaggle dataset acknowledges the Bitstamp API as its source. The Bitstamp API page states: "Bitstamp allows the incorporation and redistribution of our exchange data for commercial purposes. This includes the right to create ratios, calculations, new original works, statistics, and similar, based on the exchange data", and directs companies using the exchange data for their own commercial purposes to sign a commercial Data License Agreement (partners@bitstamp.net).
    • Jena (#6): the file is taken from the Keras / TensorFlow mirror of the MPI-BGC data; the licence (CC BY 4.0) is stated on the MPI-BGC data download page.
    • Household (#3): CC BY 4.0 is stated on the UCI dataset page.
    • ETTh1 (#1): permission from the authors to share synthetic series would lift the restriction above; it has not been requested.

The series of timesfm (series/<ds>/timesfm/) are published with the notice that they are outputs of TimesFM 3.0 and may not be used for commercial or production purposes (TimesFM Non-Commercial License v1.0); the dataset terms above apply to them as well.
